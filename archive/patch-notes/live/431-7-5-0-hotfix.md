# 7.5.0 | Hotfix

![CA_DBD_1223_Zodiac_Patch_Notes_Assets_Release_FORUM.png](../../images/73503f026fff358f-ca-dbd-1223-zodiac-patch-notes-assets-release-fo.png)

*This patch is a hotfix available on Steam only. This does not affect Steam players' ability to crossplay with other platforms.*

## Bug Fixes

- Fixed an issue which caused the Alan Wake Chapter to appear unavailable in the store.

## Known Issues

- Description text for Begrimed Chains, Filthy Slippers, Compound Seven, and The Onryo's Power do not match their in-game effects.
