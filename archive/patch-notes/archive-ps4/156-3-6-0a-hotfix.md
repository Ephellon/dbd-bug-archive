# 3.6.0a | Hotfix

## Bug Fixes

- Fixed an issue that could cause Auric Cell pack DLCs to not be consumed & converted to in-game currency. All previously purchased packages will now be properly converted during the login process after the title screen.

**Note**: If you purchased an Auric Cell pack and did not receive your Auric Cells, please be sure to fully close, update, and then restart the game.
