## AI TL;DR

Deathslinger’s chain winch break, Trapper’s bear-trap score events, Quick Draw achievement progress, Saboteur’s locker hook-aura visibility and healing interactions for Solidarity, Autodidact and For the People were all corrected on Xbox One. The hotfix resolves the chain winch not breaking after shooting, restores score reporting when resetting a bear trap, prevents dual achievement credit, lets Saboteur see hooks while locked, and fixes full-heal failures when those supportive perks are used on teammates.

# 3.6.2 | Hotfix

## Bug Fixes

- Fixed an issue that would award progress to both the Killer and the Survivor for the achievement Quick Draw.
- The Deathslinger: Fixed an issue that caused the Deathslinger's chain winch to not break after shooting.
- The Trapper: Fixed an issue that wouldn't award any score event when resetting a bear trap.
- Fixed an issue that caused Survivors with the perk Saboteur to see the hook auras while being in a locker.
- Fixed an issue that caused Survivors to be unable to be healed to full health when using the perks Solidarity, Autodidact and For the People on other Survivors.
